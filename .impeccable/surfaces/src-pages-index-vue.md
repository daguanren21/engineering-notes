# Surface brief — 工程手记 home

Visitor mode: Read.
Primary target: `src/pages/index.vue`
Related: article layout, tags, site chrome.
Seed key: `14089419`

Audience: engineers who arrive from a link, read one judgement, and come back when the same problem returns. Phone commute and desk both matter.

Job: feel that a new issue has come out, then enter the cover story.

Action: open 第 13 期封面文章《自托管机器：把工具执行留在内网》.

Proof: issue number, published date, source title, and the cover judgement itself.

Constraints: static SSG, complete with JS off, Chinese + Latin, no invented claims, digest pipeline and `flow` diagrams stay.

## Direction contract

**THESIS.** The home page is the current issue of a small technical periodical laid open under a desk lamp. It refuses the notebook-plus-featured-card and the three-card blog index.

**OWN-WORLD.** Fluorescent paper. Carbon ink. One blue reserved for action. The issue numeral is EB Garamond, large enough to be the architecture. No rust, no cream paper, no hairline column grid, no rounded cards, no cobalt field.

**STORY.** The visitor understands a new issue exists, believes the cover judgement is worth an evening, and opens it. Back issues are a bound run below the fold, not a card wall.

**FIRST VIEWPORT.** Quiet masthead: 工程手记, 第 13 期, 2026.09.10. Below it, the cover story title at display scale, then the one-sentence judgement. A single text link 阅读本期. No kicker above the title. No hero statement competing with the issue.

**FORM.** An issue spread on a light table. Signature interaction: the issue numeral is a permanent edge mark; opening an article turns the sheet, it does not navigate a site.

**QUALITY BAR.** Craft of a real printed issue: registration, ink density, folio, and the courage of one oversized numeral. Raised from the cutting-bench challenger: state is a mark (folio, date, issue) not a hue change. Raised from the cathode-gauze challenger: unused issues remain present as quiet folios, not deleted.
